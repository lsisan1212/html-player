💬 Subtitles

Click 💬 to open the subtitle picker: search by file name or track name, then click to mount it.

Load one or several .srt / .vtt files at once (multi-select); every file stays available in the picker. MKV embedded subtitles (ASS/SRT) are auto-detected with their track names.

Use the CC dropdown for quick switching, or press C to cycle. Press V to cycle 10 subtitle styles.

🔊 Audio Tracks

Movies with multiple audio tracks (dual audio, e.g. Cantonese + Mandarin) show a 🎵 selector in the control bar once loaded.

MKV/MP4/MOV audio tracks are listed with language and codec. In Chrome/Edge/Firefox the selected track is extracted and played in sync (AAC / MP3 / Opus / FLAC). Safari switches tracks natively.

AC-3 / E-AC3 / DTS tracks are marked ⚙ and decoded in software by ffmpeg.wasm (loaded on demand, ~32MB the first time). A progress overlay shows the conversion; playback switches automatically when ready.

Press A to cycle audio tracks.
